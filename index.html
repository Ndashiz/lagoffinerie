Questions before you call

The answers I give on every first call.

How long does a project take?

Most projects take between three and six weeks from the first call to the handover, depending on the integrations. You see the progress every week and validate each step.

How much does it cost?

One fixed price, agreed in writing before anything starts, right after the free call. No subscription, no per-user fee. The only recurring cost is hosting: a few euros a month, paid directly to the host.

What do I need to provide?

Your texts and photos (or the notes we take together on the call), your logo if you have one, and access to the tools the site must talk to, such as your calendar or Odoo. I handle everything else.

I already have a website. Can you start from it?

Yes. I keep what works (content, domain name, ranking) and rebuild what holds you back. Your address does not change, and the old site stays online until the new one is ready.

Do I need Odoo?

No. Odoo is one example of what the site can talk to. It works just as well with your calendar, your invoicing tool or a simple spreadsheet, and it works without any of them.

Who does the work?

I do, from the first call to the handover. No agency, no subcontracting: one person who knows your project.

What happens after the delivery?

One month of general support is included, and any defect is fixed free for six months. Anything new is quoted first as a small change request, so there are never surprises.

Who owns the site?

You do: the code, the data and every access. You can host it elsewhere, hand it to someone else or change it yourself, with or without me.

How does payment work?

A deposit when we start and the balance at delivery, both on invoice. Prices are quoted excluding VAT.

In which languages?

French, English and Dutch for our exchanges. The site itself can be in one language or several.
