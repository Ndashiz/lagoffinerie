Before you commit

Your questions. Clear answers.

Quote, timing, changes, ownership and results.

01Is the quote really free?

Yes. The 30-minute call and the quote are free, and you commit to nothing. The quote sets out the scope, the price and the schedule, in writing. Once accepted, the price does not move.

02How much does it cost?

Three packages. Essentiel: €700 excl. VAT, then about €20/month for hosting and the domain. Pro: €1,000, then about €50/month with monitoring and maintenance. Custom: on quote. The exact price is fixed in writing after the free call. Payment: a deposit when we start and the balance at delivery, on invoice.

03How long does a project take?

Most projects take between three and six weeks from the first call to the handover, depending on the integrations. You see the progress every week and validate each step.

04What if I want to change something along the way?

That is planned for. Since you validate every step, adjustments happen early. Anything outside the written scope gets a small quote before it is done: nothing is billed without your agreement.

05Who owns the site?

You do: the code, the data and every access. You can take it anywhere, hand it to someone else or change it yourself, with or without me.

06What results can I expect?

The ones we set together on the call: more enquiries, less admin, or both. You follow visits and enquiries in your own dashboard. And I never promise figures that nobody can honestly guarantee.
