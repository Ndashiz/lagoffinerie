Questions before you call

The answers I give on every first call.

How long does a project take?

Most projects take between three and six weeks from the first call to the handover, depending on the integrations. You see the progress every week and validate each step.

How much does it cost?

A showcase site starts at €1,950 excl. VAT, a site connected to your tools at €3,900 excl. VAT. The exact price is set in writing after the free call and does not move afterwards. After delivery, you run the site yourself, or I keep it running with maintenance from €29/month (hosting, proactive monitoring, response within one working day); the full plan is on quote. Work outside the plan: €85/h. Estimate your project in two minutes →

What do I need to provide?

Your texts and photos (or the notes we take together on the call), your logo if you have one, and access to the tools the site must talk to, such as your calendar or your invoicing tool. I handle everything else.

I already have a website. Can you start from it?

Yes. I keep what works (content, domain name, ranking) and rebuild what holds you back. Your address does not change, and the old site stays online until the new one is ready.

Do I need Odoo?

No. Odoo is one example of what the site can talk to. It works just as well with your calendar, your invoicing tool or a simple spreadsheet, and it works without any of them.

Who does the work?

I do, from the first call to the handover. No agency, no subcontracting: one person who knows your project.

Anyone can ask AI for a website these days. Why trust yours?

Fair question. AI makes me fast, but fast is worthless if your site goes down on a Saturday or leaks your client list. I spent the last six years shipping digital solutions for major actors across Belgium, where downtime and data leaks are not an option. Every build comes with that discipline: monitored and updated for you if you choose the maintenance plan, protected against the common attacks, a secured and backed-up database, and a documented, tested handover.

What happens after the delivery?

Five hours of maintenance are included, to use within three months, and any defect is fixed free for six months. After that, you run the site yourself or take the maintenance plan. New features always get a small quote first.

What does maintenance cover?

Keeping what exists working: technical issues, interface issues, updates, and anything that breaks on its own. Response within one working day. It does not cover change requests or new features, which are quoted separately, nor repairing damage caused by changes you make to the code or technical settings, billed separately at the hourly rate (€85/h).

Who owns the site?

You do: the code, the data and every access. You can take it anywhere, hand it to someone else or change it yourself, with or without me.

How does payment work?

A deposit when we start and the balance at delivery, both on invoice. Prices are quoted excluding VAT.

In which languages?

French, English and Dutch for our exchanges. The site itself can be in one language or several.
