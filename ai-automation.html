Before you commit

Your questions. Clear answers.

Tools, existing site, morning digest, outages and price.

01Do I need Odoo?

No. Odoo is one example of what the site can talk to. It works just as well with your calendar, your invoicing tool or a simple spreadsheet, and it works without any of them.

02Which tools can you connect the site to?

Your calendar, your invoicing tool, your CRM, your mailbox or a simple spreadsheet. If a tool can share its data, we can plug it in. I confirm it during the free call, before any quote.

03Can you automate a site I already have?

Yes. I can plug your current site into your tools, or build the automation separately, without touching the site. The price is fixed in writing after the call.

04What exactly is the morning digest?

A short summary, received every morning, written by AI from your messages and client exchanges: what came in, what is waiting for an answer, what is urgent. In two minutes, you know where to put your energy.

05What happens if a tool changes or goes down?

With maintenance, proactive monitoring alerts me and I fix it, with a response within one working day. Without maintenance, I can step in outside the plan, at €85 an hour.

06How much does it cost?

Automations are included in the connected site, from €3,900 excl. VAT. To automate an existing site or a single tool, it is on quote, with a price fixed in writing that does not move.
