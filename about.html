Who's behind it

Simon Goffin — founder

Passionate about digital transformation and about getting solutions delivered. The bridge between IT and business in large companies, now at the service of SMEs across Europe.

For years, my job has been to stand between IT and the business in large companies: the person who turns what a team needs into an application that actually ships. I have been in charge of several applications from end to end — user design, integration architecture, testing, budgeting.

To perform in a highly demanding environment, I built my own tools to automate the demanding tasks that were easy to automate. “Automate the boring, dominate the important” became my motto, and it is exactly what I bring to a small business.

Today I deliver digital solutions as a complementary independent for SMEs across Europe. I frame, I build, I connect your tools and I hand you the keys: one person who knows your project from the first call to the handover, and who still picks up the phone six months later.
