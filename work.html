Case study — Electrician

"My website brings in clients, and invoices send themselves."

Bravo Reno ↗︎Electrician & locksmith · Wallonia & Brussels
Odoo CRMBlitzAI daily digestEnquiry routing

The situation. A modern web presence to build, and an owner drowning in admin: quotes, invoices and an inbox he could not keep up with between two jobs.

What I built. A custom website connected to his real tools: an Odoo CRM integration so every enquiry lands in his client file, a Blitz integration that creates the invoice automatically when a job is done, and every request from the site routed straight to his own inbox.

On top of that, a daily report reads through his unread e-mails and sends him one short digest every morning, so even on days he cannot open them one by one, he knows exactly what to prioritise.

Case study — Volunteer syndic

The co-ownership on autopilot.

LazySyndic ↗︎Co-ownership management platform · in production
Transactions & categoriesFinancial reportsMeeting invitationsE-signature

My client is a volunteer syndic. To simplify and automate the running of the co-ownership, we built him a website that brings every task of the syndic's job into one place.

The platform lets him, among other things:

  1. Manage the financial transactionsTracking of the transactions made on the payment and reserve accounts. Each transaction is automatically assigned to an expense category, which makes the bookkeeping easy to follow.
  2. Generate financial reports automaticallyReports that sum up all the transactions, including the detailed balances of the co-ownership's accounts, so that co-owners get a clear view of the financial situation.
  3. Automate the invitations to general meetingsThe convocations to the general meetings are sent automatically, which simplifies their organisation and avoids oversights.
  4. Centralise the decisions and conclusions of the meetingsThe conclusions and decisions taken at the general meetings are recorded, with a built-in e-signature tool so the documents are signed directly online.
