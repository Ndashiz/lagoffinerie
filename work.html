Case study — Electrician

A modern site the client runs himself.

Bravo Reno ↗︎Electrician & locksmith · Wallonia & Brussels
ContentfulHostingLocal SEOAutomatic leadsGoogle Analytics

Bravo Reno is the website of a professional specialised in electrical and locksmith services. For this project, we built a modern, classic public site together with a complete administration interface, so the client manages the content and the running of his site himself.

The main features we put in place:

  1. Content the client edits himself (Contentful)Through a simple, intuitive administration interface, the client changes every piece of content on his site without technical or complicated tools.
  2. Hosting of the siteWe host the site directly on our own servers, to guarantee its availability and take care of its maintenance.
  3. A local SEO campaignA search-engine optimisation campaign to improve the site's visibility in the Huy area and its surroundings.
  4. Contact requests handled automaticallyWhen a visitor uses the contact form, the client automatically receives an e-mail and a lead is created automatically, which makes following up prospects easy.
  5. Google AnalyticsThe site is equipped with analysis tools to follow visitors' interactions, their behaviour and the performance of each page.

Case study — Volunteer syndic

The co-ownership on autopilot.

LazySyndic ↗︎Co-ownership management platform · in production
Transactions & categoriesFinancial reportsMeeting invitationsE-signature

My client is a volunteer syndic. To simplify and automate the running of the co-ownership, we built him a website that brings every task of the syndic's job into one place.

The platform lets him, among other things:

  1. Manage the financial transactionsTracking of the transactions made on the payment and reserve accounts. Each transaction is automatically assigned to an expense category, which makes the bookkeeping easy to follow.
  2. Generate financial reports automaticallyReports that sum up all the transactions, including the detailed balances of the co-ownership's accounts, so that co-owners get a clear view of the financial situation.
  3. Automate the invitations to general meetingsThe convocations to the general meetings are sent automatically, which simplifies their organisation and avoids oversights.
  4. Centralise the decisions and conclusions of the meetingsThe conclusions and decisions taken at the general meetings are recorded, with a built-in e-signature tool so the documents are signed directly online.

Case study — IT company

Team estimation, turned into a game.

Scrum Poker ↗︎Estimation tool for the team of an IT company
Scrum PokerFibonacci sequenceShared estimatesScore history

An online interface that lets the members of a team estimate their upcoming tasks together through an interactive game.

The game, based on the principle of Scrum Poker, lets each team member propose an estimate of a task's complexity using the Fibonacci sequence. The estimates are then compared to encourage discussion and reach a shared estimate.

The platform also lets team members see the estimates of the others and a history of scores, to follow previous estimates and gradually improve their accuracy.

The request came from a team lead at an IT company who wanted to simplify and structure the way his team estimates its tasks.
